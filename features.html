Features Overview

Fifteen modules in one suite, grouped by what they do.

Scatter core

  • Core scatter workbench — scatter across Unity Terrains and 3D Mesh Colliders with multi-surface raycasting; real-time 3D holographic ghost meshes preview placement in the SceneView before you commit.
  • Atomic single-step undo — one Ctrl+Z reverts a full scatter operation, even one that placed more than 1,000 objects.
  • Smart auto-populate scanner — classifies the 3D models already in your project in one click.
  • Placement rules — Poisson minimum spacing, slope angle limits, normal alignment, terrain splatmap layer masking and exclusion volume colliders.

AI and natural language

  • Multi-provider AI engine — Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, xAI Grok and OpenRouter, plus an offline fallback keyword parser and local Ollama models.
  • Procedural biome presets — seven calibrated profiles: Desert, Snow & Tundra, Swamp, Ancient Ruins, Industrial Compound, Sci-Fi Colony and Forest Floor, each enhanceable with AI narrative detail.
  • AI Design Coach — a dockable conversational assistant in the editor; click Apply Suggestion to write its recommended parameters into your active profile.
  • Variation generator — up to 10 calibrated environment variations from one base profile in one click; save any variant as a new profile asset.
  • Dungeon and room builder — interconnected multi-chamber layouts from descriptive prompts. Detailed on the Layout Builder page.

Artistic control

  • Scatter Paint Brush — paint and erase props directly onto scene colliders, with scroll-wheel radius and single-stroke undo.
  • Semantic physics filter — constrain props to spawn Near Walls, Along Roads or in Open Fields, with exclusion zones carved around doorways and player pathways.
  • Handcrafted Feel Mode — controlled asymmetry, directional dispersal and focal gap radii so clearings stay clear while micro-props cluster around anchor objects.
  • Spline road and path decorator — Catmull-Rom splines with left/right/both offsets and one-click ground snap.

Auditing and optimization

  • Density heatmap — a 32x32 color-coded SceneView overlay that flags GPU overdraw hotspots and dead zones.
  • Style consistency analyzer — detects your scene's visual style (Realistic, Low Poly, Stylized, Sci-Fi, Medieval, Horror) and warns when assigned prefabs clash with it.
  • Composition assistant — 2x2 quadrant balance, vertical silhouette diversity and focal point centroids, based on classical composition principles.
  • Platform performance budgets — five hardware tiers that clamp instance counts, widen minimum spacing and recommend LOD groups.
  • Static mesh combiner — merge scattered GameObjects into static meshes grouped by material, with 32-bit index buffers (over 65k vertices) and automatic lightmap UV generation; one-click restore returns the originals.
