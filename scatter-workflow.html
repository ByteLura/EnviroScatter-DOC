Scatter Workflow

The core workspace is the EnviroScatter Pro AI window (Tools → ByteLura → EnviroScatter Pro AI), divided into five workflow tabs.

1. Scatter tab — core parameters

  • Target Surface & Bounds — select a mesh or terrain, set padding, and position the boundary box.
  • Prefab List — assign prefabs to scatter; adjust each prefab's Weight (frequency), random rotation range and scale variation.
  • Alignment & Offsets — Normal Alignment (0 = upright, 1 = snapped to the surface slope) and Surface Y offset.
  • Filter Toggles — slope filters, height bounds, spacing rules and collision avoidance, each switched independently.

Generate a preview, tune the parameters against the accepted/rejected markers in the SceneView, then apply. Everything lands under a [Scatter] parent with full undo support.

2. AI Prompt tab — text to preset

  • Type a prompt such as "A dense, mossy redwood forest floor with boulders".
  • The window shows visual warning boxes if your profile lacks prefabs.
  • Auto-Apply (on by default) applies the AI's settings, generates a preview and instantiates the objects in one operation; you can toggle this off to review first.
  • A two-row, eight-button Quick Launch Bar in the footer opens every other window (Paint Brush, Variations, Storytelling, style consistency, composition, settings and more) in one click.

3. Biome tab — procedural presets

  • Choose from 7 pre-configured biomes: Forest, Desert, Snow/Tundra, Swamp, Ruins, Industrial or Sci-Fi.
  • Apply Biome Preset loads the preset's default constraints into your profile.
  • Enhance Biome with AI lets the active AI provider add narrative micro-details (for example campfire debris in ruins) and scatter them automatically.

4. Heatmap tab — placement density

  • Bake Heatmap from Current Preview overlays a 32x32 color-coded grid in the SceneView.
  • Color tiers: Blue (empty), Green (low), Yellow (balanced), Orange (high), Red (hotspot).
  • Diagnostic reports detect dead zones and performance hotspots and render a segmented horizontal distribution bar.

5. Performance tab — budget constraints

  • Select one of 5 platform tiers: Low-end Mobile, Mid Mobile, Console (Nintendo Switch), Console (PS5/Xbox) or High-end PC.
  • The tab displays density reduction multipliers, minimum-spacing requirements and LOD suggestions.
  • Apply Performance Budget to Profile recalculates and clamps your profile's parameters to the tier. Tier values are listed on the Performance & Mesh Combiner page.
