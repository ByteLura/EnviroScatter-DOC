Storytelling & Handcrafted Feel

This module (Tools → ByteLura → AI Storytelling & Feel) fights the robotic, procedural-spam look with narrative themes and organic scattering behavior.

Procedural storytelling

Instead of tweaking raw parameters, describe the vibe of your scene:

  • Story presets — 10+ standard presets, including Military Checkpoint, Abandoned Factory, Ancient Dungeon, Haunted Graveyard, Sci-Fi Lab Corridor and Cozy Village Square.
  • Narrative prompt — write a custom description, for example "A rustic camp with logs and campfires scattered near a stream".
  • AI generation — the active provider interprets the narrative and configures density, spacing, Y-offsets, height and slope limits to match.
  • Local fallback — offline, the tool parses prompt keywords: "abandoned" yields low density, high spacing and negative Y-offsets; "jungle" yields high density and small spacing.

Handcrafted Feel Mode

Bake manual layout biases directly into your scatter profiles:

  • Asymmetry Strength — adjusts boundary padding dynamically. Low strength keeps items strictly aligned within bounds; high strength spreads them in organic, uneven boundaries.
  • Focal Gap Radius — clears a circular breathing zone around the center of your selection bounds, keeping roads, campfires or boss arenas free of clutter. An orange wireframe disc draws the gap in the SceneView.
  • Clustering Bias — adjusts minimum spacing behavior. Negative values repel objects for an even distribution; positive values attract them into natural clusters such as grass clumps or rock piles.
  • Natural Jitter Seed — an independent secondary seed applies micro-position jitter without disrupting the overall layout balance.
  • Reset — returns all feel parameters to defaults instantly.
