Placement Tools

Beyond boundary-based scattering, two interactive tools place assets directly in the SceneView.

Scatter Paint Brush

Open it at Tools → ByteLura → Scatter Paint Brush. It paints prefabs from your active profile onto any collider in the scene with natural brush strokes.

  • Activate — click ACTIVATE BRUSH; a circular preview indicator follows your mouse in the SceneView.
  • Paint — click and drag with the left mouse button on any scene geometry.
  • Erase — hold Shift while dragging to erase previously painted objects inside the brush radius.
  • Resize — scroll the mouse wheel while the brush is active to scale the radius.
  • Density & Strength — sliders set how many objects are painted per stroke and their placement probability.
  • Spacing safeguard — the brush obeys your profile's spacing constraints, preventing overlapping trees and rocks.
  • Session statistics — tracks the total objects painted this session; undo or redo strokes, or delete all painted objects at once.

Road / Path Decorator

Open it at Tools → ByteLura → Road Path Decorator. It places props — street lights, fences, rocks, flowers — along a spline path defined by waypoints, with continuous tangential alignment.

  • Waypoints — click + Add Camera Position to drop a waypoint at your current viewport focal point, or type coordinate vectors manually. Edit or delete individual points in the list; an orange line in the SceneView shows the path.
  • Offsets — Left and Right Offset values position props alongside the path instead of directly on it.
  • Placement side — Left, Right, Both or Alternating.
  • Spacing along path — controls the distance between placed props.
  • Ground snapping — vertical raycasts at calculated points along the path align props to the surface normal.
  • Clear Decoration — removes all placed spline decorations in one click.
  • Undo — the whole placement is a single undo transaction: one Ctrl+Z reverts it completely.
