Honest Limits

What EnviroScatter Pro AI does not do, stated plainly so you can judge the fit before you build on it.

Editor-only tool

Every system in this package runs in the Unity Editor. Nothing is included in player builds — which means zero runtime overhead, but also that scattering, AI generation and combining all happen at authoring time. There is no runtime or procedural generation in a shipped game from this package.

Cloud AI needs your own account

The cloud providers (Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Grok, OpenRouter) run on your API keys and your internet connection. Any usage costs charged by those providers are yours. EnviroScatter Pro AI includes no cloud credits.

Offline AI is narrower

  • The built-in Local Offline Parser works from keyword heuristics. It is instant and free, but it cannot match a real language model's understanding of a complex description.
  • The Layout Builder requires a cloud provider: its room-graph generation queries the language model, so it does not run on the Local Offline Parser.
  • Ollama runs real models locally, but generation quality and speed depend on your hardware and the model you choose.

Placement still obeys your scene

  • Scattering is raycast-based: target surfaces need colliders, and results depend on your bounds, height filters and spacing settings. Very high minimum spacing inside small bounds will reject most candidates — the tool reports this rather than overriding your rules.
  • Boundary padding is clamped to 40% of the bounds size to prevent zero-area placement.
  • The engine audits assigned prefabs: a prefab containing a Directional Light, Camera or AudioListener is blocked from generation with a warning, rather than scattered by accident.

Performance budgets are clamps, not magic

The five platform tiers cap density and widen spacing to fit a hardware target. They do not optimize your prefabs themselves — heavy meshes and materials still cost what they cost. The mesh combiner reduces draw calls for static props; it is designed for the scattered, static environment, and originals are preserved so you can restore and re-tweak them.

Analysis tools advise, they do not decide

The style analyzer, composition assistant and heatmap report what they measure — style tags, balance scores, density tiers. They flag mismatches and hotspots; the artistic call remains yours.
